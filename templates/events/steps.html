Understanding the Graphical Method

Learn how to solve linear programming problems graphically

Steps to Solve

Define Variables

Let x = number of guests and y = number of entertainment units

  • Total Budget = B
  • Cost per Guest = Cx
  • Cost per Entertainment Unit = Cy
  • Minimum Guests Required = Gmin
  • Minimum Entertainment Units = Emin

Write Constraints

The problem is subject to these constraints:

  • Budget Constraint: Cx⋅x + Cy⋅y ≤ B
  • Minimum Guests: x ≥ Gmin
  • Minimum Entertainment: y ≥ Emin
  • Non-negativity: x, y ≥ 0

Plot Constraints

Draw the following lines on a coordinate system:

  • Budget line: Cx⋅x + Cy⋅y = B
  • Minimum guests line: x = Gmin
  • Minimum entertainment line: y = Emin

Identify Feasible Region

The feasible region is the area that satisfies all constraints:

  • Above the minimum entertainment line
  • Right of the minimum guests line
  • Below the budget line

Find Corner Points

Identify the corner points of the feasible region:

  • Point at minimum guests and minimum entertainment
  • Intersection of budget line with minimum entertainment line
  • Intersection of budget line with minimum guests line

Find Optimal Solution

Based on the objective:

  • For maximum guests: Choose point with largest x-coordinate
  • For maximum entertainment: Choose point with largest y-coordinate
  • For balanced approach: Normalize and weight both objectives equally
